AC Coach – a coach for Assetto Corsa
====================================

INSTALLATION (any Windows 10/11 PC, any user)
  Option 1 – INSTALLER (recommended): run AC_Coach_Setup_<version>.exe (e.g. AC_Coach_Setup_1.8.2.exe).
    - Installs for you only (the current Windows user), no administrator rights needed,
      into  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\AC Coach .
    - Start menu shortcuts (folder "AC Coach"): AC Coach, Manual, Uninstall,
      and optionally "AC Coach" on the desktop. At the end the installer starts AC Coach.
    - Uninstall: Start menu or Settings > Apps. Your laps and reports stay in Documents.
    - Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (the installer has no digital
      signature): click "More info" > "Run anyway".
    - Every other user of the PC installs AC Coach for themselves, with their own account.

  Option 2 – NO INSTALLATION (portable folder, e.g. on a USB stick):
    1. Extract the WHOLE "AC Coach" folder anywhere. Do not run files straight from the
       ZIP window – use "Extract all" first.
    2. Run "0. First run.bat" once – it unblocks the files, checks .NET
       Framework 4.8 and opens AC Coach. After that simply open ACCoach.exe.
    - Data is saved in the same folder as ACCoach.exe (you can move it together with
      your driving history). Read-only folder -> data goes to Documents\AC Coach.

THE AC COACH WINDOW – everything in one place
  At the top: the "Start trainer" / "Stop trainer" button, the status (waiting for the
  game / driving) and the car and track. The trainer starts by itself when the window
  opens (you can switch that off in Settings).
  Four tabs:
  DRIVE
    - Status: best lap time, last lap, focus turn.
    - Lap time target (see LAP TIME TARGET below).
    - Voice and beeps – applied AT ONCE, also while driving, and remembered:
      braking beeps + racing line cues, voice tips after turns (off = lap times and notes
      only, good with CrewChief or in a race), focus on one turn (0 = the trainer picks),
      mute everything, voice, voice and beep volume, speech rate, last beep (learn your
      reaction or a fixed number of seconds), "Test voice and beeps" button.
    - On the right the TRAINER LOG: lap times, voice tips (blue), beeps (grey),
      off-tracks and late braking (orange), targets and focus (yellow), new records
      (green), tyre and wheel data.
  ANALYSIS
    - Pick the car and track, click "Analyse last session" – the report appears in the
      window and the track map opens in your browser. "Open track map" shows the latest
      map, "Reports folder" opens the folder with the reports.
  SETTINGS
    - Folders: where to save data (empty = default) and the Assetto Corsa folder
      (empty = find it automatically; "Detect" button). Click "Apply folders".
    - Start the trainer as soon as AC Coach opens; open the map after the analysis.
    - In-game app: install it into Assetto Corsa and pick what it shows (see below).
    - Language / Język: English or Polski (applied after a restart).
    - Everything is saved automatically, separately for every Windows user:
      %APPDATA%\AC Coach\ustawienia.ini .
  HELP
    - This manual.
  A full-screen game covers the window – switch with Alt+Tab, play in borderless window
  mode, or keep AC Coach on a second monitor.

WHEELS AND TYRES (from version 1.4, no add-ons – data straight from the game)
  The trainer also records: slip and load of every wheel, tyre slip angle, car rotation
  (yaw), tyre core and brake temperatures, pressures, TC and ABS activity.
  (Laps recorded earlier do not have this data – they work as before.)
  WHILE DRIVING – after a turn the voice adds what the tyres did:
      "front wheels locking" / "rear wheels locking"   – always (a lock-up ruins the tyre),
      "wheelspin on exit", "rear stepping out", "front washing out", "traction control..."
                                                       – when the turn cost time.
  At the line: "tyres cold" (colder than on the best lap), then "tyres warm".
  The trainer log shows the temperatures and pressures of every lap.
  REPORT – "WHEELS AND TYRES" section: in which turns and how often there were lock-ups,
  wheelspin or slides, what it costs and what to change; tyre temperature at every lap
  and a "(cold)" mark.
  HTML MAP – charts: slip ratio (front/rear), slip angle (front/rear), car rotation,
  tyre temperatures and pressures; diamonds on the map where the lock-ups, wheelspin and
  slides happened. The "Where it is on the track" map shows this data too.
  Needs Assetto Corsa 1.14 or newer (older versions do not report slip).

IN-GAME APP (from version 1.8) – on the screen of the game itself
  AC Coach puts a small "AC Coach" app into Assetto Corsa (assettocorsa\apps\python\ACCoach)
  and switches it on (Documents\Assetto Corsa\cfg\python.ini). No Content Manager or
  Custom Shaders Patch needed – it also works full screen.
  SWITCH IT ON ONCE: on track move the mouse to the right edge of the screen, open the app
  bar and click "AC Coach". Drag it where you want it; the game remembers the place.
  If it is not on the list: AC launcher > Options > General > UI Modules (or Content
  Manager > Settings > Assetto Corsa > Apps / Python apps) – tick "ACCoach".
  WHAT IT SHOWS (everything from your best run of every turn, like the beeps):
    - T3   85 m   92 km/h – metres to the braking point of the next turn (from about 3 s
      before it), negative after you pass it; the apex speed of your best run.
      It moves with your speed (faster = earlier) and with the safety margin.
      After you press the brake: OK (within 5 m) / EARLY 12 m / LATE 8 m.
    - B bar – how hard to brake right now (a reaction time ahead), the white line is your
      pedal. Red BRAKE HARD -> yellow EASE OFF (trail braking) -> green LET GO.
    - G bar – throttle: green FULL THROTTLE, yellow FEED IN, orange LIFT.
    - -0.21 – time to your best lap (green = faster). From the second lap on.
  The data is updated by AC Coach as you get faster, so keep AC Coach running.
  With no laps yet the app says "no data for this car and track yet".
  Settings tab > In-game app: switch the parts on/off, "Install / update now".
  Problems: Documents\Assetto Corsa\logs\py_log.txt (lines "AC Coach").
  CHECK IT AFTER DRIVING: Analysis tab > the track map, card "In-game app" next to the charts.
    It replays exactly what the app showed at every metre of the selected lap: the same box as
    in the game, a "Play the lap" button (0.25x-2x), the chart "in-game app: metres to the
    braking point", the app's bars as yellow dashed lines on the brake and throttle charts and,
    on the big map, yellow squares where the counter reached 0 and the verdicts (T1 OK, T2 LATE 8 m).
    Data: <car>__<track>\aplikacja_w_grze.txt (the same as the game reads; written by the trainer).

LAP TIME TARGET (e.g. from 1:39.5 to 1:35)
  On the Drive tab type a time (e.g. 1:35.000 or 1:35:500) and click "Set target".
  The target is remembered for this car and track:  <car>__<track>\cel.txt .
  IS IT REALISTIC – the trainer builds a CAR MODEL from your laps: how much grip in the
  turns and how much braking the car held (not short kerb spikes) and how it accelerates,
  at every speed. Then it "drives" your line at those limits and compares with the target:
      realistic           – you already have it in your best turns, or it is within the
                            grip you already reach,
      ambitious (+X%)     – it needs X% more grip and braking than your maximum so far,
      very ambitious / very far – intermediate goals first.
  The model only knows what you have driven – the faster you drive, the better it judges.
  The real limit of the car can be higher (in the Huracán ST at Vallelunga professionals
  do about 1:31–1:33 in real life).
  INTERMEDIATE GOALS – the trainer does not ask for 1:35 straight away. First the ideal
  lap (your best turns), then about a second at a time. After every new record it moves
  to the next one and says e.g. "Intermediate goal 1 37.5".
  WHAT THE VOICE SAYS – about both the best lap and the target:
      "Turn 4, slower by 0.1, to target 0.4, apex faster by 8"
      "Turn 6, faster by 0.4, on target"
      "Time 1 38.9, minus 0.6, to target 0.7"
  The trainer log shows at every turn: "target +0.45 (total +1.62)".
  REPORT AND MAP – "LAP TIME TARGET" section: verdict, intermediate goals and a plan per turn:
      T1  -0.95 s   apex 133 → 162 km/h  [entry -0.42, exit -0.53]
      T7  -0.48 s   approach 197 → 228 km/h (330 m before the apex), apex unchanged
  (how much time and where exactly: the apex, or when the apex speed stays the same, the
  spot where the plan is fastest, e.g. a lift on the approach; in brackets the split over
  approach, entry and exit)
  A target set while the trainer is stopped applies to the car and track you drove last.

  TIPS
  - Assetto Corsa is found automatically: the running game, Steam (all libraries, every
    drive) and the usual folders. If that fails, pick the game folder on the Settings tab
    (without the game the track map has no edges, everything else works).
  - Voice: the trainer uses a Windows voice in the app's language. If there is none, it
    speaks with the default voice. Add one: Settings > Time & language > Speech >
    Add voices, then pick it on the Drive tab.
  - Works with any Windows region settings (numbers always follow the app's language).

QUICK START
  1. Open AC Coach (Start menu shortcut or ACCoach.exe) before or after starting the game.
     The trainer starts by itself and waits for the game.
  2. Drive. The trainer records every lap and coaches you by voice.
  3. After driving: "Analysis" tab > "Analyse last session".
  4. Ask Claude to "analyse" – you will get tips for the next run.

  Works offline and online. Stop: "Stop trainer" or close the window.


HOW THE TRAINER LEARNS
  - Every lap is saved in the folder  <car>__<track>\laps
  - Your best VALID lap is the reference (best lap). Everything is compared with it.
    The first clean lap = the first reference; drive faster -> new reference.
  - Invalid lap: 3 or more wheels off track, or a drive through the pits.
    It does not become the reference, but it is saved.
  - The TARGET for every turn = your FASTEST run through that turn across all laps
    (also invalid ones, if you stayed on track in that turn). Together that is the
    "ideal lap" – the trainer gives its time at the start. Beeps and tips aim at that run,
    so you improve your best time instead of just repeating it.

  AFTER EVERY TURN (about 60 m after full throttle) you hear right away how it went
  compared with your best lap:
      "Turn 3, faster by 0.12"
      "Turn 3, slower by 0.2, braking too early, by 15 metres"   <- what to fix
      "Turn 3, even"
      "Turn 3, faster by 0.15, turn record"   <- fastest ever
  - The trainer log also shows "total" at every turn: time lost/gained over the lap so far.
  - Beeps play together with the voice – they interrupt nothing, so no tip gets lost.

  PEDALS AND STEERING – only what really cost time or ended off track on your laps:
      "rear sliding, gentler throttle"   – you countersteer on the exit
      "throttle and brake together"      – both pedals before the apex
      "trail the brake deeper" / "too long on the brake" – you release the brake clearly
                                           differently from your best run
  Not spoken any more (the data showed they do not cost time): throttle under heavy
  braking, steering corrections, lifting, coasting. The measurements are still in
  analysis.json.


TRAINING MODE – BRAKING BEEPS
  Before every turn with braking you hear 3 beeps in an even rhythm:

      beep ...... beep ...... LAST  <- start braking here

  The last beep also tells you HOW HARD to brake (as on your best lap):
      high, long           = full brake
      medium, shorter      = medium, about half brake
      two low "tick-tick"  = light, or a short stab

  - Catch the rhythm: the first two beeps are the countdown, your foot goes on the brake
    with the last one.
  - The trainer log shows every beep as [BEEP 1/3], [BEEP 2/3], [BRAKE].
    You see these lines but hear no beeps? It is a sound problem – try
    "Test voice and beeps" (Drive tab) and the ACCoach volume in the Windows Volume Mixer.
  - Faster than on your best lap -> it beeps earlier; slower -> later.
  - Right after you press the brake you hear:
      "Late"   – brake clearly (over 0.35 s) after the last beep,
      "Early"  – brake clearly before the last beep.
    Brake before the last beep -> the countdown stops, no beeps while braking.
  - The trainer MEASURES your reaction at every braking and logs it, e.g.:
      beep -> brake: +0.17 s | braking point: +8 m
    (+8 m = you pressed the brake 8 m past the point the beep aimed at).
  - The trainer LEARNS your reaction time: the last beep plays as much earlier as it
    usually takes from the beep to your foot on the brake (0.10–0.60 s),
    SEPARATELY FOR EVERY TURN once it has 4 brakings there (e.g. you react slower in fast
    turns). The log shows "Last beep 0.17 s before the braking point" and the turns with
    a different setting.
  - A summary of your reactions (median, distance from the point in metres, how often
    too late / too early) is in the report and on the track map.
  - After a turn the trainer says "brake harder" or "braking too hard" when the braking
    force clearly differs from your best lap.

  SAFETY MARGIN
  - Go off in a turn -> next time the last beep plays earlier there:
    +10 m after an off-track on entry, +5 m after one on exit (30 m at most).
    The trainer says e.g. "Turn 5, next time I will beep 10 metres earlier".
  - Every clean run through that turn takes 3 m off, until the margin is gone.
  - The margin is kept between sessions: file  <car>__<track>\margines.txt
    (delete it to reset).

  FOCUS MODE ("Focus on one turn" on the Drive tab)
  - At the start the trainer picks the turn where you lose the most (average loss,
    off-tracks, what your best run has shown) and says why, e.g.
    "FOCUS: turn 4 (average +0.65 s, off-tracks 1/26 ...)".
  - The voice talks about that turn only: before it, what to fix
    ("Turn 4: apex too wide"), after it, the result and the streak ("good 1 of 3").
  - 3 good runs in a row (not slower than the best lap, no off-track) = turn mastered,
    the trainer moves to the next one by itself.
  - Beeps work in every turn. Lap times are spoken too.
  - A particular turn: type its number next to "Focus on one turn".

  Racing line cues (also in training mode only):
  - "Wider" – on the straight before a turn, when you are too close to the inside.
  - After a turn: "apex too wide", "too tight", "use the whole exit".
  - The line = your line from the best lap. "Inside/outside" means the side of the turn,
    not left/right. In an online fight simply ignore the line cues.

  Want to set it by hand? On the Drive tab untick "learn my reaction time" and type
  a number of seconds, e.g. 0.30 (that much before the braking point the last beep plays).


AFTER DRIVING
  - "Analysis" tab > "Analyse last session" saves a report in  <car>__<track>\reports
    and opens the track map (file mapa_....html in the same folder).

  - The report (and the map) has these parts:
      FOR THE NEXT RUN – the 3 turns that cost the most (average loss + off-tracks),
        for each: where you go off and why, and what you do differently in your FAST runs
        through that turn compared with the slow ones (e.g. "full throttle 88 m earlier").
      TURNS – one line per turn: loss, consistency, off-tracks (entry/exit).
      BRAKING BEEPS – how you hit the braking point, only turns that need attention.
      LAPS – time, where you went off and where you lost the most.
  - All detailed measurements (pedals, steering, every turn of every lap) are in
    analysis.json – for an analysis with Claude ("analyse").

  TRACK MAP
  - Real track edges and the AI RACING LINE (dashed green) from the game files.
    Blue = your best line.
  - The selected lap is coloured: red = brake, green = full throttle,
    yellow = partial / no throttle. Change the lap in the list at the top.
  - Blue bar = braking point of your best lap, yellow circle = last beep,
    red dot = where you actually braked, red X = off track.
  - On the right: tips and the list of turns (beep -> brake for each).
    Click a turn (on the map or in the list) to zoom in.
    Mouse wheel = zoom, drag = move, ⤢ = whole track.
  - Every turn driven without an off-track counts for comparisons – also from laps made
    invalid by another turn (only the turn with the off-track and the next one are left out).


RIVAL (e.g. a friend from the server)
  - Put the server's results file (.json, the one the server writes after a session)
    into the folder  AC Coach\rywale . The trainer picks the fastest driver in the same
    car on the same track by itself.
  - While driving, after every sector: "Sector 1, plus 0.6 to the rival", and at the line
    "..., to rival plus 1.3". The trainer log shows exactly: your sector, the rival's
    sector (from their best lap, best and average) and your best sector.
  - The report and the map have a RIVAL section: a sector table (best lap, best sectors,
    average) and conclusions – where you lose the most and in which turns.
  - The sectors are the same as on the server – the trainer reads the timing lines from
    the track files and saves them in  <car>__<track>\sektory.txt .

  COMPARISON ON THE MAP (HTML report)
  - "sectors vs rival": the selected lap is coloured on the map by sectors –
    green faster than the rival, yellow up to 0.3 s slower, red more.
  - Sector table: the rival (best lap, best sectors, average) and your lap with the
    differences. Below it all laps of the session – click one to select it.
  - "Compare with": charts along the track – speed, time difference (where you lose
    and gain), throttle, brake, steering. Solid line = selected lap, dashed = comparison.
  - Next to the charts is the "Where it is on the track" map – always visible, also when
    you scroll down. Hover over a chart (brake, throttle, steering, speed): the map zooms
    to that spot by itself and shows the turn (entry / apex / exit) and the values.
    Click a chart to pin the point; the ← → arrows move it by 10 m (Shift: 50 m).
    A small overview of the whole track sits in the corner. Zooming can be switched off
    under the map.
  - The server file only has the rival's sector TIMES. To compare their DRIVING
    (line, speed, pedals), the rival has to drive with AC Coach and give you their laps:
    put the .csv files from their  <car>__<track>\laps  folder into
    AC Coach\rywale\<name>\ . Then they appear in the "Compare with" list, and their
    line on the map (purple).

NOTES (your own reminders before a turn)
  Create the file  <car>__<track>\notes.txt , one line per turn, e.g.:
    T3: Brake at the 100 board
    T8: Short stab on the brake
  The trainer reads the note on the straight before that turn.
  Changes apply from the next lap.


GOOD TO KNOW
  - VOICE ON TRACKS WITH MANY TURNS: a turn call is said only while it still makes sense –
    a newer one replaces an older one, and once you reach the next turn the old one is
    dropped. When the next turn is close, the voice says a shorter version (e.g. just
    "Turn 7, slower by 0.2"). The full numbers are always in the log.
  - AFTER AN OFF-TRACK the voice says only "Turn 9, off track" (and maybe "I will beep
    5 metres earlier") – no comparisons, which mean nothing after an off or a spin. The
    same for the next turn when you arrive there much slower than on your best lap.
  - Laps are recorded in every session: online, offline practice, career, hotlap.
  - The window's log is saved in the data folder:  log.txt  (the run before:
    log_prev.txt). When something does not work, send that file.
  - Turns are numbered in order along the track (T1, T2...), which may differ from the
    official numbering. The numbering is FIXED – saved in  <car>__<track>\zakrety.txt
    (number and apex metre). Delete that file and the trainer numbers the turns again.
  - Every beep is logged in  <car>__<track>\pikniecia.csv  (when it beeped, when you
    braked, force, off-track) – the analysis and the map use it.
  - With CrewChief switch off "Voice tips after turns" so the voices do not overlap.
  - The "zrodla" folder is the program's source code – not needed for driving.
    Rebuilding ACCoach.exe on any Windows (no Visual Studio): zrodla\build.bat
    Installer: instalator\ACCoach.nsi (NSIS 3).
  - Advanced options (a shortcut to ACCoach.exe with parameters):  --data "folder"
    (another data folder),  --ac "game folder"  (path to Assetto Corsa),  --lang en|pl .
